This manual keeps the workspace focused on action.

01

Accounts & movements

Add balances manually, then log income, expenses or transfers. Your overview calculates the total from the current account balances.

02

Investments

Track what you own and its value. Vaultfolio does not execute trades and does not connect to a broker in this version.

03

Subscriptions

Add the billing amount, cadence and next renewal. Monthly and yearly costs are calculated automatically.

04

QR import & share

Use the QR button on an account to export its non-sensitive record. Import that code on another device. Camera scanning uses your browser only; paste is always available.

05

Your privacy

Your records are stored in this browser's local storage. No bank credentials, card numbers, or trade instructions are collected or sent by this app.

06

Live bank connections

Real bank or brokerage connections require a regulated provider and a secure authorization flow. Do not use a QR code as a substitute for bank authentication.
